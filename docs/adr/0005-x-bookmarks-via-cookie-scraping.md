# X bookmarks via cookie-session scraping

Bookmarks are pulled by scraping X with the user's session cookies on a
daily schedule, bootstrapped by a manual export, accepting ToS and
breakage risk. The official API's bookmarks read is a paid tier, and the
requirements (free, automatic, own account, low volume) have no other
path. Risk is bounded by crawl frequency and by the connector being one
swappable plugin.
