#### 4. EXPAND THE CAMPAIGN (and keep

### the brand consistent)

Once the design system was locked, | introduced 9 additional scene references to expand the brand campaigns world.

Lovart Agent quickly translated those references into a broad range of explorations from fruit top views and product close-ups to lifestyle

scenes.

Because each reference carried its own strong visual language, some

early outputs began to over-weight its fonts, colors, patterns, and packaging cues alongside the composition.

![](img_p6_2.png)

200 people murder...

Some failed scenes these look cool, but totally off-brand. Sol had to redo some and abandon

afew

This wasn’t a lack of generation capability it was a creative-direction

challenge. The Agent needed a clearer hierarchy for deciding which source controlled each part of the output.

One of Lovart’s strengths is that you can combine references and steer the system iteratively. | used that flexibility to define a clearer reference hierarchy a workflow I call Brand Lock.

Brand Lock isn’t a button or a single prompt.

It’s a methodology for deciding what each reference is/isn’t allowed

to control.

The final scene must still be materially original with a different product.

not a recreated artwork
