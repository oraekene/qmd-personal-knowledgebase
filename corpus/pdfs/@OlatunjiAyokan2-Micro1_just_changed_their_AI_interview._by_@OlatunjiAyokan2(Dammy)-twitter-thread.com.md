EJ Who is ZARA?

ZARA is Micro1's AI interviewer. A lot of people get nervous when they hear "AI interview," but there's no need to panic.

She reads your CV. Expect questions based on the skills, experience and information you provided. So make sure you actually understand what's on your CV.

B Be ready for follow-up questions.

If you say you have experience in AI evaluation, data annotation, prompt engineering or another skill, don't be surprised when she asks you to explain it further.

-----

Don't exaggerate your skills. This is probably the biggest mistake you can make. Don't add skills just because they look good on your CV. If you can't explain it, don't claim it.

Know your own projects. If you mention a project, be ready to explain what you built, what your role was, the tools you used and the problems you solved.

Stay calm. It's still an interview. Take your time, listen carefully and answer the question being asked. You don't need to sound perfect.

Most importantly: be genuine. The goal isn't to convince ZARA that you know everything. It's to clearly show what you actually know and what you can do. Prepare properly and go in with confidence. Note before your Micro1 interview: You cannot do the ZARA interview with a phone. You need a laptop with at least 8-12GB RAM.
