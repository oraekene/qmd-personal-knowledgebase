The Global Talent application is an evidence exercise. The writing matters because it helps the assessor see the pattern; presentation matters because it makes the proof easy to understand; and the recommendation letters matter because credible people can validate the work. The foundation, however, is still the work itself.

If you want to apply in the future, start building and documenting the

record now. Track the outcomes of your work, keep the evidence, and

develop relationships with respected people who can speak honestly about your contribution. When the record is strong enough, organise it into one clear and coherent story, then allow the evidence to support what you are saying.
